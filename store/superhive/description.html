Free and open, just like Blender

Blender exists because people chose to support something open that everyone can use. Sculpt-Paint Wheel follows the same spirit: it is free and open source, and anyone can download it from GitHub.

So why buy it here?

Because your purchase is what keeps it going. Sculpt-Paint Wheel is built and maintained by one independent developer. Every new Blender release changes something under the hood, and every update takes real hours of porting, testing and fixing.

When you buy Sculpt-Paint Wheel, you fund:

  • ✔ Updates for every new Blender version, so your workflow never breaks.
  • ✔ Bug fixes and polish based on real artists' feedback.
  • ✔ New features and new modes for everyone.
  • ✔ Access for everyone. Students, hobbyists and artists who can't afford to buy it still get the same tool, because people like you chose to support it.

If Sculpt-Paint Wheel saves you time, buying it here is the best way to say thanks and to make sure it is still around for your next project. Thank you! ♥
