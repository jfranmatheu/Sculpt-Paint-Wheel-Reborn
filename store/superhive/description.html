Your brushes, one keypress away.

Stop hunting through toolbars and asset shelves. Sculpt-Paint Wheel puts every brush and tool you need on a radial wheel that appears right under your cursor. Hold Space, flick toward a brush, and keep working. Your eyes never leave the model.

Made for the way artists actually work: fast, focused, and in the flow.

Why artists love it

  • Blazing-fast brush switching. One gesture instead of three clicks. Over a long session, those seconds add up to hours.
  • Muscle memory, not menus. Brushes keep their place on the ring, so your hand learns where they are.
  • Every paint mode in Blender. Sculpt, Texture, Vertex and Weight Paint, hair curves, and all Grease Pencil modes, each with its own wheel.
  • Your setup, your way. Build brush sets and custom buttons for any workflow, and switch between them in a click.
  • Feels native. Uses Blender's own brush assets, panels and operators. Nothing to relearn.
  • Ready for Blender 5.x. A modern extension, tested on Blender 5.3.

How it works

  1. Hold Space over the 3D Viewport. The wheel opens under your cursor.
  2. Flick & click. Pick a brush on the outer ring, or run a custom button in the center: stroke settings, masks, layers, remesh and more.
  3. Release Space and you're back to work.

Prefer one fluid motion? Turn on Select on Release: press, move, release, done.

Make it yours

  • Brush Sets: group your favorite brushes for each kind of task, such as blocking, detailing or hard surface, and switch sets in a click.
  • Custom Buttons: put any panel, menu or operator in the center of the wheel. Each mode comes with a list of ready-made presets, or you can add your own.
  • Your look: adjust the wheel's scale, theme and behavior in the preferences.
  • Safe updates: your sets and buttons are saved and survive updates and reinstalls.

Requirements

  • Blender 5.1 or newer, tested on Blender 5.3.
  • Windows, macOS and Linux.
  • Installs as a regular Blender extension: Edit › Preferences › Get Extensions › Install from Disk.
