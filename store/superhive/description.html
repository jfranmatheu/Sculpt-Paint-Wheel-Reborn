Your brushes, one keypress away.

Stop hunting through toolbars and asset shelves. Hold Space, flick toward a brush, and keep working. Your eyes never leave the model, and your hand quickly learns where every brush lives.

Sculpt-Paint Wheel 2 is the biggest update yet: a new ring menu, a smart centre that tunes your brush, ten themes, a double brush ring for big sets, and a cleaner, faster wheel in every sculpt and paint mode of Blender 5.

Tune your brush without leaving the wheel

Drag the centre of the wheel: up and down for the brush radius, left and right for its strength. Hold Shift for fine steps or Ctrl for big ones. Hover any button and the centre tells you what it does.

A wheel for every mode

Sculpt, Texture Paint, Vertex Paint, Weight Paint, Sculpt Curves and all four Grease Pencil modes. Each one comes with its own brushes and buttons, ready the first time you open it.

Ten themes. Or make your own.

Dark, Light, Midnight, Ember, Forest, Rose, Graphite, High Contrast, Paper and Frost. Switch in one click, tweak any color while the wheel updates live, and save your own themes.

Big brush sets, one glance

Turn on Allow Double Ring and any set that doesn't fit on one ring gets a second, staggered ring: up to 34 brushes on a single page. Smaller sets keep the compact single ring.

Make it yours

Build brush sets from any brush asset, your own included. Fill the centre ring with up to 10 buttons per set: panels, menus, operators, toggles that show their state, and buttons that run your own Python scripts. Give each button an icon and a color.

Everything at your fingertips

Already using Sculpt-Paint Wheel?

Install 2.0 over your current version: your brush sets, custom buttons and preferences are kept. Your default sets get the new default buttons, and buttons you removed stay removed.
